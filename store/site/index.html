FAFO Security

Free Advanced Features Offline. An unplug alarm for your own Android phone.

  • Arm while charging. Unplug before unlock: countdown, then a loud siren until you unlock.
  • Trigger photos kept on the phone, and optionally emailed to addresses you choose.
  • Pair your own phones over Bluetooth; circles decide who gets alerts and who can reset or disarm.
  • Intercom and push-to-talk between your own devices on the same Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
  • No ads, no tracking, no account, no server.
