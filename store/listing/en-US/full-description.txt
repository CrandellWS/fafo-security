FAFO Security turns your own charging phone into an alarm. Arm it while it is plugged in. If someone unplugs it before you unlock it, a countdown starts, then a loud siren sounds until you unlock the phone. FAFO stands for Free Advanced Features Offline: no ads, no sign-up, no analytics, and no server run by the developer.

HOW IT WORKS
• Arm only while plugged in, with your fingerprint or screen lock.
• Unplug before unlock: an adjustable warning countdown (none, 1, 5, 10, 30 or 60 seconds) beeps faster, then the full siren plays at alarm volume.
• Plug back in or unlock during the countdown to cancel it.
• The siren comes back after a restart until you unlock the phone.
• Unplug sensitivity from 0.1 to 5 seconds, for loose cables.
• Motion: a bump chirps; repeated bumps or a pickup get louder. A status light on screen goes green, yellow, orange, red.
• Optional camera flash strobe while the alarm sounds.

TRIGGER PHOTOS
• The phone takes a photo when the alarm starts, then one every few seconds (5 to 30, your choice).
• Photos stay on the phone in app-private storage. You can view, share or delete them.
• Optional: email the photos automatically to a list of addresses you choose, through any email server you set up (SMTP), or by signing in with your Google account to send through Gmail. If there is no connection, they wait and send later. When nothing else is available, the app can suggest a nearby open Wi-Fi network, which you approve once.
• Gmail sign-in is still going through Google's verification. Until it finishes, Google shows an "unverified app" screen when you sign in. The email server option works without it.

TRUSTED DEVICES AND CIRCLES
• Pair your own phones over Bluetooth. Both phones show a six-digit code to compare, and pairing needs your real screen lock.
• Put devices in circles such as Home and Work, each with its own labels and permissions.
• Trusted devices in a circle with Alerts turned on get an alert when this phone's alarm goes off, if they are on the same Wi-Fi or in Bluetooth range. A circle can be allowed to reset the alarm (siren off, stays armed) or to fully disarm it. New circles start with intercom only.
• Messages between devices are end-to-end encrypted with the paired keys and stay on your local network or Bluetooth.

INTERCOM AND PUSH-TO-TALK
• Hold a button to talk to one paired device by its label, or to all of them, on the same Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
• Room mode lets the chosen circles listen and talk back. It is turned on only by the person at that phone, shows a notification naming who is listening and the system microphone indicator the whole time, and records nothing. It can never be started remotely, and it ends when an alarm starts.

PRIVACY
• No ads, no tracking, no sign-up, and no server run by the developer.
• Your settings, devices and photos stay on your phone. Photos leave it only if you share them or turn on photo email to addresses you choose.
• A notification shows whenever the app is armed, alarming, reachable by your devices or in room mode. FAFO protects the phone it runs on; it has no hidden mode.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Someone can still turn the phone off. The siren returns when it starts again, but a phone that stays off is silent.
• Photos are not taken after a restart until you unlock the phone, because Android keeps the camera and storage locked until then.
• Alerts reach only your devices that are nearby. There is no internet alert service.
• Some phones need battery settings changed so the alarm keeps running. The app walks you through it.
• FAFO is an alarm, not a guarantee. Keep a secure screen lock and Android's built-in theft protection turned on.
